2. 보관 기간

  • 진단 결과와 이용 기록: 수집일부터 1년. 이후 삭제하거나 개인을 알아볼 수 없도록 집계 형태로만 남깁니다.
  • 상담 신청 정보: 상담이 끝난 날부터 1년.
  • Threads 게시물 기록: 수집일부터 90일. 회사가 단 답글의 기록은 중복 방지를 위해 1년.

회사는 개인정보를 판매하거나 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다. 서비스 운영을 위해 다음 업체의 인프라를 이용합니다.

  • Amazon Web Services (서버 호스팅)
  • Anthropic (진단 문장·답글 초안 생성. 가게의 공개 정보와 공개 게시물 본문만 전달합니다)

Privacy Policy (English summary)

EastLight Labs Inc. (AiBizClub) · Effective 2026-11-17

What we process

Place Check and Persona Card: the store name or Naver Place URL you enter, the store's public information, a random browser identifier, a referral tag, and your IP address (for rate limiting). Optional consultation form: name, phone, email.

ABC Reply Desk, our tool for managing our own Threads account (@aibizclub): our account's access token, and for public Threads posts found by keyword search, the post text, author username, permalink and timestamp, plus a log of replies we posted. We use this only to manage our account's posts and replies and to avoid replying to the same post or author twice. We never read private accounts or messages.

Retention

Usage records: 1 year. Consultation requests: 1 year after completion. Threads post records: 90 days (reply log: 1 year). Access tokens: deleted when disconnected or expired.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Infrastructure providers: Amazon Web Services (hosting), Anthropic (text generation, public content only), Meta Platforms (Threads API).

Your rights and data deletion

To access, correct or delete your data, or to have a reply we posted on your Threads post removed, email us with the post URL or your Threads username. We respond within 7 days.
